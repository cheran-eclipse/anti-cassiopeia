== Open Source Rover: specifications ==

top speed: ~1.6m/s (slow running, subject to motor selection)
number of motors: 10
structural material: aluminum
total cost: ~$1600 (about the cost of a TurtleBot 3 Waffle)

For a 7.2Ah battery and with some driving on rough terrain, expect to
get at least 3 hours of drive time.

When the voltage drops below 13-14V, the rover will power down.

The rover design and the software can accommodate different wheel
sizes if you find wheels you like better.
