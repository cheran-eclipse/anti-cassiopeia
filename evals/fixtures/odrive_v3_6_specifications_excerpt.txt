== Electrical Specifications ==

Besides the input voltage range, (12V to 24V for ODrive v3.6 24V, 12V to
56V for ODrive v3.6 56V), the electrical specifications of both version
of ODrive v3.6 are the same.

== ODrive v3.6 24V and 56V ==

* Peak current per motor: 120 Amps
* Heatsink in still air: 40A per channel
* Heatsink with basic fan cooling: 75A per channel
* Heatsink with overkill fan cooling: 90A per channel

.. note:: ODrive versions after v3.5 are closed-source with respect to
   board files and schematics.
